About Me

I'm Marc Lamberts — a football recruitment & data-analysis consultant. I specialise in bridging scouting, video analysis and data modelling to support clubs in men's and women's football. My fascination with patterns, tactics and metrics drives my work.

My career so far has taken me through roles in the Netherlands, Sweden and England. For example, I joined London City Lionesses as Recruitment Analyst Intern after stints in the Dutch men's game and Swedish women's top division.

I write and publish regularly on topics such as set-piece value models, expected performance metrics, and talent identification in elite and emerging markets. My aim is to translate complex data into actionable insights for clubs, coaches and players.

Whether you're looking to strengthen your recruitment pipeline, build a data-driven scouting workflow, or collaborate on a performance-analysis project, I'm always open to new opportunities. Let's explore how we can work together.
